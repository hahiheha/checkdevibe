Think before you share.

Check de-VIBE gives young people a calm, evidence-based read on what they see online: what can be checked, what is just drama, and what to do next. You keep the final call.

Misinformation moves faster than the truth

Social media is where young people get their information, and where fake news, hate trains and drama spread fastest. Most fact-checking sits at article level, far from the comments where things escalate.

70%more likely to be retweeted: false news vs. true, across 126,000 stories (Vosoughi et al., 2018)
68.8%of 1,850 Vietnamese adolescents and young adults had met fake news on social media (Nguyen et al., 2025)
↑Frequent exposure is linked to higher depression and anxiety. Many users just avoid it instead of verifying.

Who it's for

Primary: Gen Z and young adults (16–24) in Vietnam on Facebook, Threads, TikTok and YouTube.
Secondary: teenagers (13–17) worldwide on Instagram, TikTok, Threads and Snapchat.

What it aims to do

Check at the point of reading, explain why content is flagged, show a clear confidence level, and let users save a timestamped report.

Select. Review. Check. Choose.

Every check starts with you: highlight text or draw a box on screen. Nothing is scanned in the background. Tap a step to see the real prototype.

Your selection→Up to 3 claims→Tavily search per claim→Gemini compares to excerpts→Policy layer checks citations→Layered report

Accurate facts can be framed to manipulate, and lies can sound calm. That's why evidence and manipulation are scored apart. If evidence is too thin, the tool says Insufficient evidence rather than guessing.

Ten levels, not a true/false verdict

A label makes you weigh the evidence instead of just accepting an answer. Tap a level.

Private by design, open by design

Local-first

Backend runs on loopback only. Screenshots are processed in memory. No database stores your text or analyses. API keys stay outside the extension.

You are in control

It never reports, removes or hides content on its own. Helpfulness ratings (1–5) stay on your device as anonymous counts.

Open & modular

TypeScript with shared Zod contracts. Routing, retrieval, AI evaluation, policy checks, OCR and reports are replaceable parts. Mock mode works without paid API keys.

Honest limits & roadmap

Where it can go wrong

The system can make mistakes. Community reports may conflict and must be re-verified. Improving it needs a large, stable contributor base.

What's next

Vietnamese localization, accessibility, classroom exercises, clearer source explanations, structured evaluation studies, and opt-in, reviewed community contributions.
